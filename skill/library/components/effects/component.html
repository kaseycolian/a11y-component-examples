Correct example

One effect used the way it is meant to be used. The backdrop is invisible to the accessibility tree, and the text on top of it still clears contrast.

1 · Grid backdrop behind text

Order 462 shipped on Tuesday. Jordan Lee was notified the same afternoon, and Invoice 99 is still pending.

The backdrop adds no element and no role, so a screen reader cannot tell it is there. The text on top is what has to clear contrast: 16.79:1 against the surface, 15.72:1 over a grid line.

Common mistakes

Four failures, live rather than described. Three of them are invisible to axe, and the fourth passes in the light theme.

2 · Backdrop with no stacking context

isolation: isolate

isolation: auto

Both panels get the same ::before from the same rule. The second one drops isolation: isolate, so its backdrop paints behind the panel's own background and never shows — in any theme, at any width, with nothing invalid for a tool to report.

3 · Gradient bar with the tint turned up

Billing

Order 462 shipped on Tuesday. Invoice 99 is still pending.

Invoice 99 · Jordan Lee

Billing, with the tint turned up

  1. fx-bar-top — 16.79:1 at the ends, 11.35:1 at 55%
  2. fx-bar-bottom — 16.79:1 at the ends, 10:1 at 55%
  3. the same gradient at 70% accent — 16.79:1 at the ends and 2.18:1 in the middle, where 4.5:1 is the floor

A 135° gradient runs corner to corner, so all three bars are 16.79:1 at two corners nobody puts text in. That is the number a color picker lands on. Sample the stops instead.

Tab into both. The first announces its contents and nothing about itself; the second says “Recent orders, region” first. The gradient thumb is 5.2:1 against its track at its dimmest point, where a control needs 3:1 (SC 1.4.11).

5 · Motion gated by a descendant selector

fx-pulse

calc(var(--ac-motion) * 1.8s)

Both boxes carry data-motion="off" and both resolve --motion to 0. The left one keeps animating, because the rule that stops fx-pulse looks for the attribute on an ancestor. Reduce motion in the header sets it on <html> and stops both.

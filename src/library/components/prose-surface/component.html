1 · The specimen

Notes from the basement

The room on Marcy Street held ninety-nine people and the door list said 462. Nobody was counting by the second set. There is a write-up of the same night in issue four, and it disagrees with this one about almost everything.

Doors at nine

Two bands, one PA, and a generator out the back. The set list came off a napkin and half of it was crossed out before anyone played.

  • Roots & radicals, twice
  • Timebomb, cut short
  • Ruby Soho, into the fence

The archive is a flat file and the query is one line:

curl -s https://zine.example/setlists?night=462&venue=basement-marcy | jq '.songs[] | .title'

Everything past the fold is real text, in the accessibility tree, and reachable by keyboard. That is the whole component.

The surface scrolls, so it takes a tab stop, a role and a name. Data Table owns those three attributes; this page is about what goes inside.

A scroller inside a scroller is still a scroller, and needs the same three attributes the surface has. Not white-space: pre-wrap: code that wraps wherever it likes reads as different code.

5 · Why not aria-label

Curfew at the Berkeley show: 23:00.

Doors: 19:30.

The first line reads “Curfew at the Berkeley show: 23:00”. The second reads “Doors: 19:30” — the aria-label on an empty span with no role is discarded, and the markup gives no hint that it was.

3 · Long, scrolling content

Terms of service

Section 1. This drawer exists to show what happens when the content is taller than the panel.

Section 2. The body is the scroll container, so the title and the close button stay reachable no matter how far down you are.

Section 3. Scrolling here does not scroll the page behind, because the body carries overscroll-behavior: contain.

Section 4. There is deliberately more text than fits, so the scroll is real rather than described.

Section 5. The panel is capped at min(80vh, 34rem), so it never grows past the viewport on a short screen.

Section 6. The body is a flex item with min-height: 0, which is what lets it shrink below its content instead of overflowing the panel.

Section 7. Without that, the text below this point would be clipped and unreachable — the bug this example exists to rule out.

Section 8. Tab from here: focus still cannot leave the drawer.

Section 9. The close button above has not moved.

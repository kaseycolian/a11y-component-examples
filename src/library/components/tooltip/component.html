3 · Why title is not a tooltip

Never appears on keyboard focus. Times out while you are still reading. Cannot be hovered, styled, wrapped or zoomed. Unreachable on touch. Some screen readers read it, some ignore it, and some read it instead of the label. Use it for nothing you need read.

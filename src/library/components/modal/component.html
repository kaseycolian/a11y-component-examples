4 · Content longer than the screen

Billing terms

Billing period. Plans renew on the day of the month you started, and an invoice is emailed to the billing contact that morning.

Seats. Adding a teammate mid-period is charged pro rata. Removing one credits the next invoice rather than refunding the current one.

Payment. Cards are charged automatically. A failed charge is retried for seven days, and the workspace stays open throughout.

Past due. After seven days the workspace becomes read-only. Nothing is deleted, and paying restores access immediately.

Refunds. Within 30 days of a charge, to the original payment method. Partial months are not refunded outside that window.

Changing plan. An upgrade takes effect at once and is charged pro rata. A downgrade takes effect at the next renewal.

Cancelling. Access continues to the end of the period already paid for. Export your data before that date.

Tax. Charged where required, at the rate for the country on the billing address. A VAT number moves the charge to your side.
